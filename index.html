03/The Colophon

A catalogue,
not a checklist.

There are no accounts here, no save files, no little stars to collect. Just 154 CRPGs, their covers, and the platforms that carried them.

Every cover lives in this repository rather than being pulled from a storefront. Store buttons lead directly to each game's Steam or GOG page, with no affiliate strings attached. Platform names have been normalised and brought together into seven readable families. Release dates follow the source list, warts and all.

Each entry carries two editorial scores. Quality blends critic reception with player sentiment; Popularity measures audience reach from review volume, with comparable estimates for GOG-only titles. They are reading aids, not verdicts.

The shelf is adapted from the long-running r/CRPG recommendations wiki . It is a love letter, not a checklist.
